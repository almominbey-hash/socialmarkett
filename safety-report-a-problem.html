How to report a problem

3 min read

If something is wrong, tell us quickly. The sooner we know, the more we can do.

If the problem is with an order

  1. Open your orderGo to Orders and open the order.
  2. Press Report a problemThis button is there until we release the payment (24 hours after delivery). The payout stops.
  3. Tell us what happenedWrite what is wrong. Add details like what was promised and what you got.
  4. Our team looks into itWe contact both sides. If the seller did not deliver what was promised, you can get a refund.
Do not wait

After the hold ends, the seller is paid and it becomes much harder to help. Check the account straight away.

If the problem is something else

For a suspicious person, a fake listing, a message that scared you, or anything else, send us a ticket. Choose Trust & safety as the topic.

  • Add the order number (it looks like SM-123) if you have one.
  • Add the username or listing name.
  • Tell us in simple words what happened, and when.
