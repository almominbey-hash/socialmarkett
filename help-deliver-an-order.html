How to deliver an order

3 min read

When a buyer pays, the order starts. Deliver quickly and clearly. Fast delivery means happy buyers and faster payouts.

  1. Check the orderYou will see a notification and the order in your Seller Dashboard. The payment status must say Paid.
  2. Talk to the buyerOpen Messages and agree on how and when you will hand over the account.
  3. Hand over the accountGive the buyer everything they need, such as the login details and the linked email or phone. Keep a note of what you gave.
  4. Mark as deliveredIn your Seller Dashboard, press Mark delivered. The 24-hour hold starts now.
  5. Wait for the buyerThe buyer checks the account and confirms. When the hold ends, your payout is released.
Do not be late

If a paid order is not delivered after 72 hours, it shows as Late. Late orders can be cancelled and refunded.

Good habits

  • Keep all talk inside our Messages. It helps us if there is ever a problem.
  • Do not take the money or the chat outside the site.
  • Do not keep access to the account after delivery.
