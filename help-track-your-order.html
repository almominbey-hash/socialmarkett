How to track and confirm your order

2 min read

After you pay, you can follow your order from start to finish in the Orders page.

  1. Open OrdersSign in and choose Orders at the top of the page.
  2. Read the statusYou will see steps such as Payment received, Order delivered and Buyer confirmed. A short message under the order tells you what to do next.
  3. Talk to the sellerUse Messages if you need the account details or have a question.
  4. Check the accountWhen the seller marks the order as delivered, log in and check everything.
  5. Confirm or reportIf all is good, press Confirm received. If not, press Report a problem. You can report until we release the payment, 24 hours after delivery.
Late orders

If a paid order is not delivered after 72 hours, it shows as Late. Send us a ticket and we will help.
