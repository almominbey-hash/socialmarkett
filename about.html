About us

AVIBIX is a safe place in Pakistan to buy and sell social media accounts.

What we do

Buying an account online is risky. People get cheated, and honest sellers are not trusted. We built AVIBIX to fix this with three simple ideas.

  1. Every seller is checkedWe check each seller's ID by hand, and we check every listing.
  2. Your money is held safelyWe hold the payment until the buyer has checked the account.
  3. Real people help youIf something goes wrong, our team looks into it and helps both sides.

Built for Pakistan

Prices are in PKR and sellers are paid to local payout accounts.
