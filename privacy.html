Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

socialmarket ("we", "us", "our") is a website where people buy and sell social media accounts. This policy explains what information we collect when you use our website, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

When you sign in with Google: your name, email address and profile picture, as provided by your Google account. We do not receive your Google password, and we do not access your Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google data.

When you sign up with email: your name, email address and password. Passwords are stored in encrypted (hashed) form and we cannot read them.

If you become a seller (identity verification): your full name, phone number, ID type and ID number (for example CNIC or passport), photos of the front and back of your ID, and your payout details (bank account or PayPal email).

When you list an account for sale: the platform, niche, title, description, follower count, income figures, price, account link and screenshots you provide.

When you use the site: messages you send to buyers or sellers, accounts you save, and basic technical data such as browser type and the pages you open.

2. How we use your information

  • To create your account and let you sign in.
  • To verify sellers and help prevent fraud and fake listings.
  • To show your listings to buyers and let buyers and sellers message each other.
  • To process orders and send payouts to sellers once payments are available.
  • To contact you about your account, orders or support requests.
  • To keep the website secure and to improve it.

Our use of information received from Google follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. We use Google sign-in only to identify you and create your account.

3. Identity documents

ID photos and ID numbers are collected only from sellers, only for verification, and are stored in private storage. They are never shown to buyers or to other users. Only our team can view them, and only to verify you or to investigate fraud.

4. Where your data is stored

Your data is stored with our service provider Supabase (database, sign-in and file storage). Our website files are hosted on GitHub Pages. Google provides the "Continue with Google" sign-in. These providers process data on our behalf to run the service.

5. Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it for advertising. We share information only:

  • with the service providers named above, so the website can work;
  • with a buyer or seller you are dealing with, limited to what is needed for the sale (for example your display name and the messages you send);
  • when the law or a valid legal request requires it, or to protect our users and rights.

6. Cookies and browser storage

We use cookies and browser storage only to keep you signed in and to make the site work. You can clear or block them in your browser settings, but you may then be signed out or some features may not work.

7. How long we keep your data

We keep your information while your account is active and for as long as needed to run the service, prevent fraud and meet legal obligations. When you ask us to delete your account, we delete or anonymise your data, except what we must keep by law or to resolve disputes.

8. Your rights

You can ask us to show you the information we hold about you, correct it, or delete your account and data. Contact us using the details below and we will respond within a reasonable time.

9. Security

We take reasonable technical steps to protect your information, including encrypted connections and private storage for ID documents. No method of storing or sending data over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot promise absolute security.

10. Children

socialmarket is not for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
