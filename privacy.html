Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Your privacy matters to us. This page explains what personal information socialmarket collects, why we collect it, who can see it, and what you can do about it. We do not sell your personal information.

1. Who we are

socialmarket is a website where people buy and sell social media accounts. In this policy, “we”, “us” and “our” mean socialmarket. This policy should be read together with our Terms of Service.

2. What we collect

When you create an account

  • your full name and email address;
  • your password (we do not see it in readable form; it is stored in a protected, scrambled form by our login provider);
  • if you choose “Continue with Google”, the name and email that Google shares with us. We do not get your Google password.

When you become a seller (verification)

  • your full name as on your ID and your phone number;
  • your ID type (national ID card or passport) and ID number;
  • photos of the front and back of your ID;
  • your payout details (bank account or PayPal email) so we can pay you.

When you use the site

  • Listings: platform, account link or username, niche, followers, income, price, title, description and screenshots you upload;
  • Messages you send to other users through socialmarket;
  • Saved accounts you add to your list;
  • Orders and payment records when online payment is available (amounts, dates, status, and the details needed to send payouts or refunds);
  • Technical information that your browser sends automatically, such as your IP address, browser type and device type, and the pages you open. Our service providers may keep this in their server logs for security and to keep the site working.

If you contact us by email, we keep your message and our reply.

3. How we use it

  • to create and run your account and let you log in;
  • to check that sellers are real people and to prevent fraud, scams and stolen accounts;
  • to review and show listings, and to let buyers and sellers talk to each other;
  • to hold payments, send payouts and handle refunds and disputes;
  • to send you important messages, such as a password reset link, the result of a listing review, or a notice about an order;
  • to keep the site safe, fix problems and improve it;
  • to follow the law and answer valid requests from authorities.

We use your information only for these purposes. We do not use it for advertising and we do not show third-party ads.

4. Who sees your information

Other users

Live listings are public. Visitors and buyers can see the platform, niche, title, description, numbers, price and screenshots of a listing, and the seller’s first name with the first letter of the last name (for example “Ali K.”) and whether the seller is verified. Buyers cannot see the seller’s account link, ID details, phone number or payout details. When you message another user, they can see your name and the messages you send in that chat.

Our team

Only the people who run socialmarket can see seller verification details and ID photos, and only to verify you, prevent fraud and handle payouts or disputes. Your ID photos are kept in a private storage area that the public cannot open.

Others

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only:

  • with the service providers listed below who help us run the site;
  • with payment providers or banks when needed to take a payment, make a payout or issue a refund;
  • when the law requires it, or to respond to a valid request from a court or authority;
  • to protect the rights, safety and property of our users, the public or socialmarket, for example to stop fraud;
  • if socialmarket is ever sold or merged with another business, in which case your information may move to the new owner, who must respect this policy.

5. Services we use

We use trusted companies to run the site. They process information for us under their own privacy rules:

  • Supabase – our database, login system and private file storage (accounts, listings, messages, ID photos).
  • GitHub Pages – hosts the website files. GitHub may record your IP address when you visit.
  • Google – if you choose Google sign-in, and to load the Inter font from Google Fonts. Your browser contacts Google when the page loads.
  • jsDelivr – a content delivery network that serves part of our site code.
  • Payment providers – when online payments open, the provider we choose will handle card, bank or wallet details. We will not store full card numbers ourselves.

Links to other websites, such as our social media pages, are not covered by this policy. Please read their privacy policies.

6. Cookies and storage

We do not use advertising cookies or tracking tools. To keep you logged in, our login system saves a small login token in your browser’s local storage. This is needed for the site to work. You can remove it any time by logging out or by clearing your browser’s site data. If you do, you will need to log in again.

7. How we protect it

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. Connections to the site are encrypted (HTTPS), the database uses access rules so that users can only reach their own data, and ID photos are in private storage. Even so, no website can be 100% secure, and we cannot promise absolute security. Please use a strong, unique password, never share it, and log out on shared devices. If you think your account is at risk, contact us straight away.

8. How long we keep it

  • Account information: for as long as your account is open.
  • Seller verification details and ID photos: while you are a seller, and afterwards for as long as we need them to prevent fraud, handle disputes or follow the law.
  • Order and payment records: as long as the law or our accounting needs require.
  • Messages and listings: while your account is open, or until you ask us to delete them, unless we need them for a dispute or the law.

When we no longer need information, we delete it or make it anonymous.

9. Your choices and rights

You can:

  • see and change your name and basic details in your account;
  • ask us for a copy of the personal information we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct information that is wrong;
  • ask us to delete your account and information. We may need to keep some details if the law requires it, or to settle an open sale, dispute or fraud check;
  • remove saved accounts or delete your pending listings yourself;
  • object to or limit certain uses of your information, where the law gives you that right.

To use any of these rights, email us at the address below. We may ask you to prove it is really you before we act. We will reply as soon as we reasonably can.

10. Children

socialmarket is not for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you think a child has given us personal information, please tell us and we will delete it.

11. Other countries

Our service providers may store and process information on servers in other countries. By using socialmarket you understand that your information may be handled outside the country where you live. We choose providers that protect information with reasonable safeguards.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the date at the top, and for important changes we may also tell you on the site or by email. Please check this page now and then.

13. Contact us

For any question about your privacy or to use your rights, write to us at YOUR-EMAIL@EXAMPLE.COM.
