Common questions

How do I know a seller is real?

We check every seller's ID by hand, and we check every listing before it goes live.

Will you ever ask for my password?

Never. Anyone who asks for your password or login code is trying to cheat you.

Someone asked me to pay outside the site. What should I do?

Say no and stop the chat. Then send us a ticket so we can look at that person.

How do I report a problem?

For an order, press Report a problem on the order. For anything else, submit a ticket and choose Trust & safety.

What happens after I report?

Our team looks into it and contacts you. For an order, the payout is paused until we decide.

I think I was scammed. What now?

Do not send more money. Keep screenshots and send us a ticket straight away. The sooner we know, the more we can do.

Nothing found. Try another word, or send us a ticket.

Something looks wrong?

Send us a support ticket. We reply inside your account, so you can follow the whole conversation.
