Seller questions

Does it cost money to list an account?

No. Listing is free. We keep 8% of the price only when your account sells.

How long does approval take?

We check new sellers and new listings by hand. It usually takes up to 24 hours.

When do I get paid?

After you deliver, we hold the money for 24 hours. If the buyer does not report a problem, we send your payout to your payout account.

Can I change my payout account?

Yes. Send us a ticket and we will help you change it safely.

Why was my listing rejected?

Usually the screenshots are unclear, the numbers do not match, or the account link is missing. Fix it and submit again.

What if a buyer reports a problem?

We pause the payout and contact you. If the account was as described, we release your money.

Can I take the buyer outside the site?

No. Keep all talk and payments on the site. It protects both of you.

Which ID can I use?

A National ID card (CNIC) or a passport. The name must match what you type in the form.
