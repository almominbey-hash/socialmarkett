How to check an account before you buy

3 min read

A few minutes of checking can save you a lot of money. Use this list on every account you like.

Your checklist

  1. Read the whole descriptionLook at what the account is about, how old it is and what comes with it.
  2. Study the screenshotsCheck that the dates are recent and that the followers, views and income match the numbers in the listing.
  3. Ask the seller questionsUse Messages. Good questions: How did the account grow? Why are you selling? Does it have any warnings or strikes? Will you give me the linked email or phone?
  4. Check the sellerOpen the seller profile. See how many sales they have and what other buyers said.
  5. Compare the priceLook at similar accounts. A very low price is a warning sign. Read How to avoid scams.
  6. Keep the chat on the siteNever pay or talk outside. Your payment is only protected inside the site.
Check again after delivery

When you receive the account, check it once more. Compare it with the listing. Only then press Confirm received.
