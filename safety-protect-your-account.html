Protect your account after you buy

3 min read

The first hour after delivery is the most important. Take these steps straight away so the old owner cannot get back in.

  1. Change the passwordUse a long, new password that you have never used before.
  2. Change the email and phoneReplace the old owner's email and phone number with your own.
  3. Turn on two-step loginAdd an extra security step, such as a code on your phone.
  4. Log out other devicesUse the "log out everywhere" option in the platform's settings.
  5. Check linked apps and payment detailsRemove anything you do not know. Add your own payout details if the account earns money.
  6. Then confirm the orderPress Confirm received only after you are sure everything works.
Keep your proof

Take screenshots of the account before and after. They help us a lot if there is a problem.
