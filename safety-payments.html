Pay safely: what to do and what not to do

3 min read

Your payment is only protected when you follow the normal steps on the site. These simple rules keep your money safe.

Do

  • Start every order from the listing page and pay with Pay now.
  • Keep every message and every payment step inside the site.
  • Check the amount and the order number before you pay.
  • Keep your payment receipt until the order is finished.

Do not

Never do these things

Do not send money directly to a seller. Do not pay "a little extra to be faster". Do not pay someone who says they work for us and asks for money in a chat or on social media. Do not share a payment code or a password with anyone.

If you already paid and something looks wrong

  1. Do not send more moneyStop and do not pay again.
  2. Keep proofTake screenshots of the chat, the order and your payment.
  3. Tell us quicklySend a ticket with the order number. If the order is open, also press Report a problem.
