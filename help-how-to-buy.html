How to buy an account safely

3 min read

Buying here is simple. Your money is held safely until you confirm that you got what you paid for.

  1. Find an accountBrowse by platform, audience, price and followers. Use the filters to narrow the list.
  2. Look closelyRead the description and check the analytics screenshots. Ask the seller questions in Messages before you decide.
  3. BuySign in and start the order. Pay using the options shown. Your payment is held by us, not sent to the seller.
  4. Receive the accountThe seller hands over the account and marks the order as delivered.
  5. Check everythingLog in, look at the numbers and make sure it matches the listing. Then press Confirm received.
  6. DoneAfter a short hold, we pay the seller. If something is wrong, press Report a problem before the hold ends.
Never rush

Take your time in step 5. Once the seller is paid, it is hard to get the money back.
