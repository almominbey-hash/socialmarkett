How ID verification works

3 min read

We check every seller so buyers can trust the people they buy from. It takes a few minutes to fill in and usually up to 24 hours for us to review.

What you will be asked for

  • Your full name, exactly as on your ID
  • Your phone number
  • Your ID type (National ID card or passport) and ID number
  • A clear photo of the front of your ID
  • A clear photo of the back of your ID
  • Your payout details, which is where we send your money
Your ID is private

Your ID photos are stored privately. Only our team can see them, and only to verify you. Buyers never see them.

Step by step

  1. Open the formSign in and choose Become a Seller.
  2. Type your detailsUse the same spelling as your ID card. A different spelling is the number one reason for a rejection.
  3. Take clear photosUse daylight. Keep all four corners in the picture. Make sure the text is sharp and nothing is covered.
  4. Add your payout detailsDouble check the numbers. Your money is sent here after each sale.
  5. Submit and waitYou will see Your application is in review. We usually finish within 24 hours.

If you are rejected

Do not worry. You will see a message. Fix the problem and apply again. Most people pass the second time.

Why do you need my ID?

It protects buyers and honest sellers. It stops people from selling accounts they do not own.

Can I use someone else's ID?

No. The ID, the name and the payout account must belong to you.
