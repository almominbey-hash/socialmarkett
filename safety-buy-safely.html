How your payment is protected

2 min read

We never pay the seller straight away. Your money stays with us until you have checked the account.

  1. You pay usYour payment goes to us and is held. The seller can see that you paid, but does not get the money yet.
  2. The seller deliversThe seller gives you the account and marks the order as delivered.
  3. You checkLog in and check the account. You can press Confirm received when you are happy.
  4. A short holdAfter delivery we keep the money for 24 hours more. You can still report a problem during this time.
  5. The seller is paidIf there is no problem, we send the money to the seller. If there is a problem, we stop the payout and look into it.
Our team looks at every complaint

If you press Report a problem, the payout stops and our team contacts you and the seller. If the seller did not deliver what was promised, you can get a refund.

Sellers are checked too

Every seller shows an ID and our team checks them by hand. Every listing is also checked before buyers can see it.
