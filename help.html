Buyer questions

Is it safe to pay on this site?

Yes. Your payment is held by us and the seller does not get it until you have checked the account. Read How your payment is protected.

When does the seller get my money?

After the seller delivers, we hold the money for 24 hours. If nobody reports a problem, we pay the seller.

What if the account is not what the listing said?

Open the order and press Report a problem before the hold ends. The payout stops and our team looks into it.

Can I get a refund?

If the seller did not deliver what was promised, our team can refund you. We look at every case one by one, so please keep screenshots.

How long does delivery take?

Sellers should deliver soon after you pay. If a paid order is not delivered after 72 hours, it shows as Late and you can send us a ticket.

Can I talk to the seller before I buy?

Yes. Use Messages on the listing. Ask all your questions before you pay.

Can I pay the seller directly to be faster?

No. Always pay on the site. Money sent outside the site cannot be protected.

How do I contact support?

Press Submit a ticket on this page. We reply inside your account.
