Fees and payouts

3 min read

Listing is free. We only charge a fee when your account sells. The fee is 8% of the sale price.

The fee

When a buyer pays for your account, we keep 8% of the price. You receive the rest. There are no monthly fees and no listing fees.

Sale pricePKR 50,000
Fee (8%)− PKR 4,000
You receivePKR 46,000

When do I get paid?

  1. The buyer paysThe money is held safely by us. It is not sent to you yet.
  2. You deliver the accountHand over the account and mark the order as delivered.
  3. The buyer checks and confirmsThe buyer makes sure everything is as described, then confirms.
  4. 24-hour holdAfter delivery, we keep the money for 24 hours. During this time the buyer can report a problem.
  5. We send your payoutWhen the hold ends and there is no problem, we send your money to the payout account you added.
Where can I see my money?

Open your Seller Dashboard. It shows what is already paid to you and what is waiting in escrow.

If the buyer reports a problem

We pause the payout and our team contacts you. If the problem is real, the buyer may get a refund. If not, we release your money. Always deliver exactly what your listing says to avoid this.

Can I change my payout account?

Yes. Send us a ticket and we will help you update it safely.

Is the fee added on top for the buyer?

No. The fee is taken from the sale price.
