How to create a listing

3 min read

A good listing helps buyers trust you and helps your account sell faster. Here is how to make one.

Before you start

You need a verified seller account. If you have not done this yet, read How ID verification works first.

Step by step

  1. Open the listing formSign in, then choose Become a Seller at the top of the page. If you are already verified, choose Create a listing.
  2. Choose the platform and nichePick where the account lives (for example YouTube or Instagram) and what it is about (for example Gaming or Food). Then pick the account type and audience that fit best. If nothing fits, choose Others and type your own.
  3. Add the account link or usernameBuyers do not see this until they buy. We use it to check your account.
  4. Add followers and monthly incomeWrite the real numbers. Income is the amount the account makes in one month, in PKR.
  5. Upload 3 to 5 screenshotsShow your analytics, such as views, followers and earnings. Clear and recent screenshots work best.
  6. Write a title and descriptionThe title must be 10 to 20 characters. The description must be at least 20 characters. Tell buyers what the account is about, how it grew and what comes with it.
  7. Set your priceEnter your price in PKR. Remember that a fee is taken when the account sells. See Fees and payouts.
  8. SubmitOur team checks every listing and emails you within 24 hours. When it is approved, buyers can see it.

Tips for a better listing

Be honest

Wrong numbers or old screenshots can get your listing rejected. They also lead to complaints after the sale.

  • Use screenshots from the last 30 days.
  • Say clearly if the account is monetized, fresh or aged.
  • Answer buyer messages quickly. Fast sellers sell more.

Common questions

Why was my listing rejected?

The most common reasons are unclear screenshots, numbers that do not match the screenshots, or a missing account link. You will see the reason. Fix it and submit again.

Can I edit a listing after I submit it?

If you need a change, send us a ticket and tell us which listing and what to change.

Do buyers see my account link?

No. The link stays private until a buyer pays and the order starts.
