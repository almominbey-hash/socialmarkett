Who can sell on this site

2 min read

Anyone can sell here if they own the account and pass a quick check. This page explains what we look for.

What you need

  • You own the account. You must be the real owner and be able to hand it over.
  • You are a real person with a valid ID. We accept a National ID card (CNIC) or a passport.
  • You have a phone number we can reach. We only use it to contact you about orders. Buyers never see it.
  • You have a payout account. This is where we send your money after a sale.

How to become a seller

  1. Create your accountSign up with your email or Google.
  2. Apply to sellChoose Become a Seller and fill in the short form. See How ID verification works.
  3. Wait for approvalOur team checks every seller by hand. This usually takes up to 24 hours.
  4. Start listingWhen you are approved, you can create listings.

What is not allowed

Please do not list

Accounts you do not own, stolen or hacked accounts, accounts with fake followers that you present as real, or anything that breaks the rules of the platform or our Terms of service. We remove these listings and can close the seller account.

If your application is not approved

This usually means a photo was not clear or a detail did not match your ID. Check your information, then apply again. If you are unsure why, send us a ticket.
