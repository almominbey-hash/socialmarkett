How to avoid scams

3 min read

Most scams use the same tricks. If you know them, you are very hard to cheat.

The golden rule

Always pay and talk inside the site

If someone asks you to pay outside, or to move the chat to another app, say no. Money sent outside the site cannot be protected or refunded by us.

Warning signs

  • The price is far lower than similar accounts.
  • The seller is in a big hurry and says the offer ends in minutes.
  • The seller will not answer simple questions or show analytics.
  • Screenshots look edited or very old.
  • You are asked to send a code or password that has nothing to do with the order.
  • Someone says they are from our team and asks for your password. We never do this.

What to do before you buy

  1. Ask questionsAsk how the account grew and why they are selling.
  2. CompareLook at other accounts of the same type and price.
  3. Read the seller profileLook at how many sales they have and what other buyers said.

If you think something is wrong

Stop the chat. Do not send any more money. Then report it to us.
