Fake messages and fake support

2 min read

Some people pretend to be our team to steal money or passwords. Here is how to spot them.

We will never

Ask for your password, your login code or your payment code. Ask you to pay outside the site. Ask you to install an app or click a strange link to "fix" your account.

Signs of a fake message

  • It says your account will be closed in a few minutes.
  • It comes from a personal number, a different email or a social media account.
  • It has spelling mistakes or a link that looks a little different from our site.
  • It promises a refund or a prize if you act now.

What to do

  1. Do not reply and do not click
  2. Check on the siteSign in on our site by typing the address yourself. Real news about your order is always in Orders and Messages.
  3. Tell usSend a ticket and add a screenshot.
